\chapter{Pricing Mechanisms in Accommodation Services}
Pricing in accommodation services is a complex decision-making problem that involves working with multiple factors. This chapter explores different pricing mechanisms necessary for effective pricing strategies in the accommodation sector. We will discuss what mechanism will be used to approach dynamic pricing, what factors influence pricing decisions. The problem is formalized in terms of relevant parameters of our revenue management case. Finally we discuss principal approach to setting prices which will be developed into a formal model in subsequent chapter.
