\chapter*{Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction}

Pricing decisions play a central role in the performance of short-term accommodation providers, as daily price adjustments directly influence both occupancy and revenue. While large hotel chains typically employ sophisticated revenue management systems supported by dedicated analytical teams, smaller accommodation providers, such as operators of short-term apartment rentals, often lack access to comparable tools and expertise. As a result, pricing decisions in this segment are frequently based on intuition or simple heuristics rather than systematic, data-driven methods.

This thesis addresses the problem of dynamic pricing for short-term accommodation providers by proposing a practical, data-driven pricing model that can be implemented using reservation data routinely available to operational managers. The model does not require access to external market data or specialized econometric expertise. Instead, it leverages the information encoded in historical booking and pricing records to forecast future demand and derive price recommendations consistent with historically observed pricing behavior. The primary goal is to propose an accessible decision-support tool that can support and partially automate pricing decisions in small-scale accommodation management.

The thesis is organized as follows. Chapter~1 provides an overview of relevant concepts in accommodation revenue management, including capacity management, price elasticity of demand, demand forecasting methods, and pricing strategies. The proposed mathematical model is introduced in Chapter~2; it combines a SARIMA-based demand forecasting stage with a probabilistic pricing stage based on kernel density estimation of the joint occupancy–price distribution. Chapter~3 presents the practical application of the model on a real-world dataset of anonymized reservation records from a segment of Prague apartments, evaluating both forecasting performance and the quality of the resulting price recommendations.
